## Landing (en inglés)
**Headline:** Find the FBA reimbursements Amazon still owes you

**Subheadline:** Upload your FBA reports. We audit lost, damaged and unreceived inbound inventory and hand you ready-to-file claim text, before the window closes. First audit is free.

**Benefits**
1. See exactly how much Amazon owes you: units, shipment IDs and estimated dollars, in one list.
2. Ready-to-file claim text with the evidence Amazon asks for (proof of ownership, proof of delivery, shipment reconcile data).
3. Deadline tracking: Amazon's claim windows are short, so we flag each claim before it expires.

**How it works**
1. Download your FBA reports from Seller Central (Inventory Adjustments, Reconciliation / shipment data) and upload the CSVs. No login or API access needed.
2. We audit them and send you a free summary: what is claimable and how much.
3. Get claim text for every item plus deadlines. File them yourself or let us track them. Flat price, no commission.

**Pricing and presale**
- US$49/month flat. Keep 100% of what you recover (no 20-25% cut).
- Founding-member presale: pay US$49 today, first month fully refundable if your free audit finds less than US$150 recoverable. Limited to the first 20 sellers.
- Free audit first. Presale is optional.

**FAQ**
1. Do you need access to my Seller Central account? No. You export the reports and upload CSVs. Nothing else is shared.
2. Why not just use a commission service? Commission services take a percentage of every dollar recovered. If you recover a few hundred dollars a month, a flat fee can cost less.
3. What does Amazon already reimburse automatically? Amazon reimburses some losses proactively, but not everything. Our audit shows what is still unclaimed in your own data.
4. What if you find nothing? Then you pay nothing for the audit and get a full refund on a presale.

2. When was the last time you filed a reimbursement claim? What happened?

## Borradores de publicaciones
**Post 1: How to manually audit your FBA reimbursements (educativo)**
> Title: A 20-minute manual check for money Amazon may owe you (inbound + lost inventory)
> 1. Download Inventory Adjustments from Reports > Fulfillment. 2. Filter adjustment codes: look for "M" (missing) with no matching "F" (found). 3. Cross-check inbound shipments in the shipping queue > Reconcile tab: received vs expected units. 4. Check claim windows: sources say most are now about 60 days; confirm in your Seller Central policy page. 5. Gather proof of ownership (invoice) and proof of delivery. Questions welcome; curious what's tripped you up.
