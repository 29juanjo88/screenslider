Nota: usa un nombre propio (ej. "PauseFlow", provisional; verifica disponibilidad).

**Headline:** Turn "Cancel" into "Pause" - keep more WooCommerce subscribers.

**Subheadline:** A smart cancellation flow for WooCommerce Subscriptions that offers a pause, skip or discount at the exact moment a customer tries to leave.

**Benefits**
1. Save would-be cancellations: offer a 1-3 month pause instead of a lost subscriber.
2. Know why people leave: a one-click reason survey feeds a simple report.
3. Set up in minutes: no code, works with your existing WooCommerce Subscriptions.

**How it works**
1. Install the plugin and pick your offers (pause, skip next renewal, % off).
2. When a subscriber clicks Cancel, they see the right offer for their reason.
3. You see saves, pauses and reasons in one dashboard.

**Price and pre-sale**

2. Does it handle failed payments? Focus is voluntary cancellations; failed-payment recovery is on the roadmap.
3. What if I don't like it? Full refund before launch, no questions asked.

## Guion de entrevista
Inglés (Mom Test):
1. Tell me about the last time a subscriber cancelled on your store. What happened?
2. How many active subscribers do you have, and how many cancelled last month?
3. Of those, how many cancelled on purpose vs. failed payments? How do you know?
4. What did you do right after the last cancellation?
5. Have you ever tried to win a subscriber back? Walk me through it.
6. Do your customers ever ask to pause, skip or delay a renewal? When was the last time?
7. What tools or plugins do you use today for subscriptions or retention? What did you pay?
8. What did you try before that, and why did you stop?
9. What's the biggest leak in your subscription revenue right now?
10. How much time or money did you spend on this problem in the last 3 months?
11. If a tool offered a pause at the cancel button, what would stop you from installing it this week?
12. I'm taking founding customers: 3 months for $59, fully refundable. Want to reserve a spot today?

Te piden pausar o saltar renovaciones. 7. Qué herramienta usas y cuánto pagas. 8. Qué probaste antes. 9. Mayor fuga de ingresos hoy. 10. Tiempo/dinero gastado en 3 meses. 11. Qué te frenaría de instalarlo. 12. Pedir la preventa ($59, 3 meses, reembolsable).
